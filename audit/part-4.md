# Audit part 4 of 5: old SJIBL videos (slice 4)

Scope: 13 videos, 138 sampled frames, all read. Live verification ran against the deployed build
(web 10.2.1.50:8080, tenant shatadal, dev-auth) as personas built from the real permission lists in
`design-contract/ui/role-catalogue.json` (store-keeper, security-officer, facilities-manager,
fleet-manager, dispenser, employee, viewer, tenant-administrator). View only, nothing created or
approved. Live screenshots are `evidence/slice4-live-*.png`; old frames are `evidence/slice4-<video>-*.jpg`.

Results: 31 issues. By severity: 9 high, 16 medium, 6 low. By live status: 27 STILL-REPRODUCES,
2 FIXED-SINCE, 2 NOT-CHECKABLE.

Two things changed since 2026-09-18 that matter when reading the old frames:
- The five list-screen failures were reworded. The old text was `query_not_a_reference: X points at
  nothing, so there is nowhere to hop to`. The live text is `X on Y does not link to another record, so
  this view cannot follow it`. It reads better, but the screen still fails to load and still names
  internal columns and tables.
- Status pills are now readable and the boot flash is reported fixed (see S4-19 and S4-20).

Persona note: the old "admin" login could open `/med/prescription/new` and `/works/boq`. The
`tenant-administrator` role from the catalogue cannot ("You do not have permission for this"). I treated
that as a difference in the login used, not as a defect.
