## Notes for whoever fixes these

- S2-01 to S2-04 are one engine behavior: a declared reference or column that the entity does not have makes the whole list refuse. The safest fix is at the list-declaration level (drop or correct the column) and to make the engine degrade to "column unavailable" rather than fail the screen.
- S2-07, S2-08, S2-10, S2-13, S2-16 and S2-17 are all "spec text used as UI copy". A single sweep that separates label text from design notes would clear most of them.
- I did not change any code, create branches or redeploy.
